## Final prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram. Asset type: production architecture illustration for KataDroid's technical web page. Create an elegant wide landscape editorial isometric infographic, approximately 3:2 composition, with a warm paper background #f7f5ef, muted sage green #dfe6d6, forest green #2d604e, terracotta #b94b27, charcoal #242921, and pale wood. The website has refined serif typography and restrained warm colors. Show the WHOLE ANDROID GO APP as a cohesive exploded layered system, rather than a generic software flowchart. Top third: a small upright Android phone displaying a clean Go board with black and white stones, accompanied by a branching game-record tree, two SGF document sheets, a settings dial, a small saved-analysis chart and a model/benchmark gauge. These elements represent Compose UI, game records, settings, saved analysis and benchmarks. Middle: neat thin rounded physical slabs, the upper engine slab labeled exactly 'KataGo', lower inference slab labeled exactly 'LiteRT'. Bottom third: three separate small hardware platforms reached by thin copper-like branching traces from LiteRT, labeled exactly 'CPU', 'QNN', and 'Neuron'; QNN and Neuron each have a little NPU chip as their hardware endpoint. Canonical short labels exactly: 'Compose' by the phone, 'SGF' by the record documents, 'KataGo' on upper middle slab, 'LiteRT' on lower middle slab, 'CPU', 'QNN', 'Neuron' on their respective hardware platforms. These labels must be crisp, spelled correctly, and each used once. No other text, no headline, no paragraphs, no performance figures, no logos, no watermarks. Keep only a few curved routing traces that make UI -> local engine -> inference backends legible. Shallow 3D objects, soft matte material, gentle ambient shadows, precise designer composition, generous negative space. Subtle grain like a high-end technical editorial illustration, not photorealistic, not neon, not dark cyberpunk, not cartoon people. The upper app layer must be visually prominent and rich enough to convey more than KataGo on a phone. Keep all objects inset from edges. Background is opaque warm cream, not transparent.
